Overview

Sanare Health — Healthcare Provider Finder, Price Transparency & Personal Health Data Platform

Sanare is a health-tech project focused on helping users find doctors, compare procedure prices, book appointments, and manage personal health data in one place. The platform combines real CMS provider data, CPT-based pricing, and an AI assistant to create a smoother, more transparent patient experience.

Project Components:
• Provider & Doctor Search (CMS Data): Built around scraped and structured CMS physician/provider data, enabling users to search by specialty, location, practice type, and distance
• Appointment Booking: Includes a booking flow that allows users to request or schedule appointments directly with nearby providers listed in the directory
• Price Transparency with CPT Codes: Integrates CPT procedure codes and associated pricing information to help users understand the cost of common procedures before receiving care
• AI Navigation Chatbot: Features an AI assistant that guides users throughout the platform, answers questions, and navigates to providers, pricing pages, or appointment booking sections automatically
• Personal Health Data Section: Contains an early prototype designed to let users store and access their medical documents (labs, summaries, etc.) in one secure location, supporting long-term ownership of their health data

Project Purpose: Sanare serves as a full-stack exploration of healthcare data challenges, provider search usability, price transparency workflows, AI-assisted navigation, and patient-centered digital design. The project reflects hands-on experience with data pipelines, healthcare interoperability, AI integrations, and user-focused product workflows.
